# Drawer polish: the drawer is the sidebar's width, its foot scrolls on a short screen, and the phone bar moves with the scroll (O307, O309, O337) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The open drawer is 14rem plus the left safe area; below 500 px tall its foot scrolls with the list; in the phone layout the phone bar moves with the page pixel for pixel, rests in the page when scrolling stops, and keeps the part of itself in view across a turn.

**Architecture:** `web/src/phoneBar.ts` (the prototype's `protoHideBar.ts`, renamed and reduced) holds the band rule as a pure function and a hook `Shell` calls once. The hook writes the track's height and the bar's rest state to the DOM from its listeners, observers, timers and effects, never through React state. The bar stays `position: sticky` inside `.phone-bar-track`, a box at the page's top whose height the hook moves, so the compositor carries the bar with the page. `scrollerSwap.ts` reads the view's top from `barLine()`; `scrollKeeper.ts` says each landing, so a navigation shows the bar after the kept scroll is put back. The drawer's changes are CSS in `shell.css`, plus one scroll in `AccountMenu.tsx` that keeps More in the column's view while its panel is open.

**Tech Stack:** React 19, TypeScript, Vitest 4 (projects `node`, `jsdom`, `browser` with two Chromium instances), CSS, Biome.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-drawer-polish-design.md` (approved 2026-11-17; its "my call" items are settled). Prototype: the branch's three "Prototype (throwaway)" commits, the last authored 2026-11-17 16:46:47 UTC, approved for feel only.

## Global Constraints

- Values, from the spec: drawer `width: calc(14rem + env(safe-area-inset-left, 0px))`; foot `position: static` under `@media (height < 500px)`; H is the bar's `offsetHeight` (`--sticky-top`, 52 px plus the top inset); on each scroll, with y clamped to `[0, maxScroll]`, `E = max(H, clamp(E, y, y + H))`; `E = y + H` while the drawer is open or `:focus-visible` is inside the bar, and at a navigation; the bar rests (`position: relative` at the same pixels, in its track's flow) whenever any of it is in view and the page is not scrolling: at `scrollend`, or 150 ms after the last scroll where `scrollend` is missing, after each show (so under the open drawer, and still once it closes), at mount, and at a turn's end; a `wheel`, `touchmove` or `scroll` leaves rest, and a `wheel` or `touchmove` with no page scroll within 150 ms rests again; a turn is an `orientationchange` or a width-changing `resize`, held until 600 ms after the last; a height-only resize is not a turn.
- The phone layout's condition comes from `phoneLayout.ts`; CSS writes `(width < 900px) or (height < 500px)` letter for letter. The foot's `(height < 500px)` is `shell.css`'s one other size query, named as an exception in `phoneLayout.css.test.ts` and in `builtStylesheet.test.ts`'s known list, each built from `PHONE_LAYOUT_PARTS`, never retyped.
- The policy runs no inline script and no inline style: the hook writes the track's height and `--rest-top` through `element.style` (CSSOM), never `setAttribute('style', …)`, and nothing is added to `index.html`.
- No React render per scroll frame: E, the rest state and the turn's hold live in the hook's closure and the DOM.
- Nothing at module level needs a browser, and the hook does nothing where `matchMedia` or `ResizeObserver` is missing (jsdom), so every jsdom suite that mounts `Shell` runs unchanged.
- Names (the builder's call, made here): `web/src/phoneBar.ts`; `.phone-bar-track`, the track, wrapping `<header className="phone-bar">` in `Shell.tsx` where the prototype put it; `data-at-rest` and `--rest-top` on the bar.
